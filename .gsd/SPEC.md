# Project Specification: Tanya's 4-Year Friendship Special 3D Birthday Web Experience

## Objective
Create a breathtaking, mobile-first, 3D animated interactive Birthday surprise web experience for Tanya, celebrating her birthday and the milestone of 4 wonderful years of friendship (1460+ days).

## Core Architecture
- **Tech Stack**: HTML5, Modern CSS3 (Glassmorphism, 3D Transforms, Shimmer effects), Vanilla JavaScript ES6+, Three.js (for 3D heart particles & ambient 3D depth), Canvas Confetti, Web Audio API (realtime synthesized procedural sound effects + music engine).
- **Mobile Experience**: 100% responsive, high DPI, touch-optimized (tap, drag, swipe, pinch), works in WhatsApp in-app browser, iOS Safari, and Android Chrome without app downloads.
- **Visual Aesthetic**: Luxury Midnight & Rose Gold theme, glowing nebulae, ambient floating 3D hearts, gold foils, polaroid aesthetic for photos.

## Milestones & Atomic Slices
1. **Slice 1 - Foundation & Project Assets**:
   - Clean folder structure (`assets/images/`, `css/`, `js/`, `index.html`).
   - Image assets mapped with high-definition rendering and fallback handling.
   - GSD specification and `progress.txt` initialized.
2. **Slice 2 - 3D Particle Canvas & Audio Engine**:
   - Three.js floating 3D hearts, stars, and golden bokeh sparkles that respond to touch gestures.
   - Web Audio synthesizer (pop, chime, cheer, blow-out, music box melody) + background music player with toggle.
3. **Slice 3 - Hero Screen & Tap-to-Unlock**:
   - Dramatic entrance: "A Special Surprise is waiting for Tanya... Tap to Unlock ✨".
   - Smooth opening transition into the main celebration hub.
4. **Slice 4 - 3D Interactive Birthday Cake & Candle Blow**:
   - Layered 3D styled cake with animated flickering flames.
   - Interactive candle blow (tap/touch) -> realistic flame extinguish, smoke, crowd cheer, confetti blast.
5. **Slice 5 - 4-Year Friendship Milestone & 3D Interactive Photo Cube / Carousel**:
   - "4 Years of Unbreakable Friendship (1,460+ Days)" animated counter.
   - 3D rotating photo carousel featuring all 5 uploaded photos of Tanya.
   - 4 Flip cards (Year 1 to Year 4) with touching, heartfelt Hindi-English notes.
6. **Slice 6 - 3D Wax-Sealed Secret Envelope & Letter**:
   - Sealed envelope with animated wax stamp.
   - Tap to break seal and reveal smooth scrolling heartfelt birthday letter.
7. **Slice 7 - Interactive Surprise Gift Box & Touch Fireworks**:
   - 3D gift box with unwrapping animation releasing floating compliments and balloons.
   - Screen-tap sparkler / fireworks effect anywhere on touch.
8. **Slice 8 - Verification, Code Review (CodeRabbit) & Polish**:
   - Cross-browser syntax & responsiveness check.
   - CodeRabbit audit for security, zero broken links, performance, and accessibility.
   - Git commits for each verified slice.
